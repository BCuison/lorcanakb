Chapter II

2026

Core and Coconut bans

Nov 3, 2026 · Announcement

Bans
My Adventure Book (Attack of the Vine) is banned in Core. Bend to My Will is banned in the Format Coconut beta only. Both take effect immediately.

Hyperia City release notes

Oct 19, 2026 · Comprehensive Rules 2.3 · Release notes

Rules
  • Ink drops arrive, with a new rules section on counters. See Newer mechanics.
  • New keyword Adventurous: can’t challenge, must quest each turn if able.
  • “Enters play” effects are now static: the card’s own effects apply first, then effects from other cards.
  • If a card gains a keyword it already has, the one that lasts longer applies.
  • Effects where every player acts at once resolve one part at a time, in turn order.
  • Choices for a move are still made even if the move won’t happen.
Errata
Jukebox’s On Repeat now says “another card” in your discard, so a song can’t count itself. Five cards were reworded to use Adventurous: Cobra Bubbles - Dedicated Official, This Growing Pressure, Ariel - Curious Traveler, Woody - Town Sheriff and Gaston - Frightful Bully.

Format Coconut beta

Aug 8, 2026 · Announcement

Formats
A new multiplayer format in open beta: up to three inks, at least 60 cards, up to 4 copies of your chosen Coconut character and 1 copy of everything else. Play to 25 lore.

Rotation

Aug 4, 2026 · Explainer

Formats
Shimmering Skies, Azurite Sea, Archazia’s Island and Reign of Jafar have left Core. Core now starts at Fabled. A card from those sets that was reprinted in Fabled or later stays legal.

Tournament Rules update

Jul 14, 2026 · Resources

Tournaments
A new version of the Tournament Rules took effect. Ravensburger didn’t post a summary of the changes.

Attack of the Vine release notes

Jul 9, 2026 · Comprehensive Rules 2.2 · Release notes

Rules
  • Dual-named characters count as each name but are one character. New Team classification. See Newer mechanics.
  • New Shift variants: Duo, Combo, Temporary, classification (such as Temporary Red Panda) and Potato Shift.
  • Shifting onto two characters in different states takes the drying or exerted state.
  • A “may” ability whose cost you can’t pay is treated as choosing not to.
  • Damage rules were gathered into one section. How damage is calculated didn’t change.
Errata
None.

Wilds Unknown release notes

May 2026 · Comprehensive Rules 2.1 · Release notes (PDF)

Rules
  • Moving damage counts as removing it and then putting it on. So “whenever you remove damage” abilities trigger on a move.
  • Moving damage isn’t dealing damage, and damage can’t be moved onto the character it came from.
  • A drying character can’t exert for any reason, including to pay a cost.
  • Modifiers apply before replacement effects, and choices for damage effects are made during the damage calculation.
  • “Beginning Phase” is now called the “Start-of-Turn Phase”.
Errata
Wording only, to say “takes damage”: Beast - Relentless, Hydra - Deadly Serpent, John Silver - Vengeful Pirate, Devil’s Eye Diamond, Maid Marian - Badminton Ace, Captain Hook - The Pirate King, Brutus - Fearsome Crocodile and Nathaniel Flint - Notorious Pirate.

Infinity ban

Mar 26, 2026 · Announcement

Bans
Hiram Flaversham - Toymaker is banned in Infinity, effective immediately.

Winterspell release notes

Feb 10, 2026 · Comprehensive Rules 2.0 · Release notes

Rules
  • Deck-out: you no longer lose for drawing from an empty deck. You lose if you end your turn with no cards in your deck.
  • New keyword Underdog: on your first turn, if you didn’t go first, pay 1 less to play this character.
  • A full rewrite of the Comprehensive Rules: turn actions such as challenging got their own step-by-step sections, plus a new section on how cards and effects resolve.
Errata
Wording only, no change in play: Belle - Strange but Special and Sail the Azurite Sea now say “you may ink an additional card”.
Chapter III

2025

Whispers in the Well release notes

Nov 17, 2025 · Release notes

Rules
  • New keywords Alert (challenge as if Evasive) and Boost. See Newer mechanics.
  • Cards under other cards aren’t in play, and putting a card under another isn’t playing it.
  • The same replacement effect can’t apply twice to the same event.
Errata
None.

Fabled release notes

Aug 18, 2025 · Release notes

Formats
The first rotation: The First Chapter, Rise of the Floodborn, Into the Inklands and Ursula’s Return left Core when Fabled released. Rotation now happens once a year. Infinity keeps every set.
Rules
  • A reprint is the same card as the original: they share copy limits, and either printing is legal wherever the card is.
  • Shift costs are now printed with the ink symbol after the number, the same way ink costs are shown elsewhere.

Core bans

Apr 8, 2025 · Announcement

Bans
Hiram Flaversham - Toymaker and Fortisphere were banned in Core. Neither is on the current Core list, and the Nov 2026 announcement doesn’t say why.
