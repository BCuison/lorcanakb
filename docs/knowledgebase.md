## 1. Rules changes to know

### Comprehensive Rules 2.0 (Winterspell, Feb 2026)
- **Deck-out:** you now lose if you end your turn with no cards in your deck.
- **Old rule:** drawing from an empty deck was an instant loss.
- **Why it changed:** timing issues, such as both players drawing from empty decks at the same time.
- **Play impact:** drawing your last card(s) mid-turn is no longer fatal; you have that turn to win.
- Source: [Winterspell release notes](https://www.disneylorcana.com/en-US/news/2026/02/winterspell-release-notes)

### Hyperia City (Set 14, Oct 2026)
- Store prerelease Oct 16, 2026; wide release Oct 23, 2026.
- Tournament legal from its prerelease (Tournament Rules 1.6), so Core is now Sets 9 to 14.
- New mechanic: **ink drops** (Comprehensive Rules 2.3.0, effective Oct 16, 2026):
  - Counters created by card effects and placed in your inkwell. Not cards.
  - Remove one to pay 1 ink toward any ink cost (playing cards, abilities, move costs).
  - No limit on how many you hold, and they can be saved for later turns.
  - 36 of the set's 204 cards create them.
- Correction: an earlier fan preview said drops must be spent the turn they're made. CR 2.3.0 says otherwise.

## 2. Keywords in depth

There are 15 keywords: Adventurous (Hyperia City, Oct 2026), Alert, Bodyguard, Boost, Challenger, Evasive, Reckless, Resist, Rush, Shift, Singer, Sing Together, Support, Vanish, Ward.

### Adventurous (Hyperia City, Set 14)
- Can't challenge, and must quest each turn if able. The opposite of Reckless.
- Five older cards were reworded to use it (Hyperia City release notes).
- Source: [Hyperia City release notes](https://disneylorcana.com/en/news/2026/10/disney-lorcana-tcg-set-release-notes-hyperia-city/)

### Alert (Whispers in the Well, Set 10)
- Can challenge as if it had Evasive.
- Alert is **not** Evasive. Effects that buff "your Evasive characters" don't apply.
- A character with Alert can still gain Evasive from another ability.

### Boost N (Whispers in the Well, Set 10)
- Once during your turn, pay N ink to put the top card of your deck face down under the character.
- Many cards gain abilities or bonuses based on cards under them.
- Appears in all six inks.
- Interaction to remember: it removes cards from your deck, which matters more under the new deck-out rule.

### Sing Together N (Ursula's Return, Set 4)
- Exert any number of your ready characters whose costs add to N or more to sing the song for free.
- "Whenever this character sings a song" triggers on each character that sang.
- Many Sing Together songs are uninkable and expensive, so they need a wide board.

### Bodyguard
- Two abilities: may enter play exerted, and opponents must challenge it first if able.

### Reckless
- Can't quest, must challenge if able. Can still exert to sing songs or use abilities.

## 3. Formats and rotation

- **Core Constructed:** rotates the four oldest sets once per year.
  - Sets 1-4 rotated out in Sept 2025 (with Fabled).
  - Sets 5-8 rotated out in July 2026 (with Set 13, Attack of the Vine!).
  - Core is now Sets 9 onward. Holds 5 to 8 sets at a time.
- **Infinity Constructed:** all sets legal, separate banlist.
- **Reprints:** a card reprinted in a Core-legal set is legal, including older printings with the same full name.
- **Tournament legality:** new sets become legal a few weeks after release. Check the event before buying singles.

## 6. Illumineer's Quest

Co-op scenarios against a self-playing villain deck. Quest list: [Illumineer's Quest](../illumineers-quest.html). Releases in order: Deep Trouble (Ursula, May 2024), Palace Heist (Jafar, Jun 2025), The Great Hunny Rescue (The Vine, Oct 2026).

### Palace Heist (Jafar, released Jun 2025)
Rules page: [Palace Heist](../quests/palace-heist.html).
Verified against the printed rules booklet (photos, 2026-10-28).
- 1 to 4 Illumineers vs Jafar's 50-card scenario deck. Normal deckbuilding rules for Illumineers.
- Win: each Illumineer at 20+ lore while one of them has the Reforged Crown. Jafar wins at 40 lore (he takes the Crown, then wins). While Jafar has the Crown he can't lose.
- Jafar's draw: 1 player 2 (+1 at 20 lore); 2 players 2 (+1 at 10 and 30); 3 players 3 (+1 at 20); 4 players 3 (+1 at 10 and 30). Bonuses are kept even if his lore drops.
- Jafar's turn: Ready, Set (Crown +2, location lore), Resolve the row (items use exert abilities; characters quest; Reckless characters that can't quest challenge, Illumineers pick the defender), Draw (facedown pile = hand), Reveal (play free if ink >= cost, else ink it). He draws on his first turn and goes first.
- Jafar reshuffles his discard when his deck runs out. Cards returned to his hand are played before new draws. Illumineers make any choices for him.
- Crown steal: Jafar has no locations; every Illumineer exerts one ready, dry character; pick one Illumineer to take it. Same steps take it from another Illumineer. Only the holder gains the 2 lore.
- Jafar retakes the Crown via Mechanical Snake, Palace Guard - Crown Hunter, Iago - Crown Seeker questing, the Crown leaving play, or reaching 40.
- Ink Moat starts in play on Normal, Hard and Extreme; on Easy it's shuffled into his deck. Jafar can't move characters to his locations.
- Battleground abilities: pay lore, each usable once per turn.
- Illumineers' turns are simultaneous. Effects naming a "player" can target Jafar.
