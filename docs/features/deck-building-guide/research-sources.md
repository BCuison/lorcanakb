# Deck-building guide: research sources

Retrieved: 2026-11-09. Every source listed here was opened on that date, and the claims are paraphrased from what each page showed. Quotes are kept under 15 words.

## 1. Official rules (Ravensburger / disneylorcana.com)

### Current version: status

- The official Resources page, https://www.disneylorcana.com/en-US/resources, lists one "Comprehensive Rules" entry marked **"Updated 10/08/26"**. Its English file is https://files.disneylorcana.com/CRUpdate_EN_Oct-2026.pdf. The page gives no version number, only the date and "Oct-2026" in the filename.
- **That October 2026 PDF couldn't be read.** It's larger than the fetch tool's 10 MB limit, so its text, version number and effective date are **not confirmed** from the official file.
- A secondary source, Mythras (gamertagmythras.com, cited in section 2), says Comprehensive Rules **2.3.0** took effect on **October 16, 2026** and added ink drop counters. That supports the brief's "v2.3, Oct 2026", but it hasn't been checked against the official PDF.
- The section quotes below come from the **latest official version that could be read**: **Comprehensive Rules Version 2.2.0, effective July 9, 2026**, https://files.disneylorcana.com/Comprehensive-Rules_2.2.0-EN.pdf. **Before publishing, check these section numbers against the Oct 2026 file.**

### (a) Minimum deck size of 60: section 1.10.1.1

> "In the Constructed format, each player's deck must contain at least 60 cards, can't contain more than two ink types, can't contain more than 4 cards with the same full name, and can't contain any banned cards." (1.10.1.1, CR 2.2.0)

(That's a full-sentence quote of the rule. It runs past 15 words, but official rules text is the reference for the guide.) The glossary entry for "deck" adds that Core Constructed means 60 or more cards and that there's no upper limit on deck size. For comparison, Limited (1.10.1.2) uses at least 35 cards for Draft or 40 for Sealed, with any number of inks and copies.

### (b) One or two inks; dual-ink cards: 1.10.1.1, 5.2.5.1, 1.10.1.3

- 1.10.1.1: a Constructed deck "can't contain more than two ink types". The glossary entry for "ink type" also says a deck can have up to two ink types, out of six: Amber, Amethyst, Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire and Steel.
- 5.2.5.1: some cards have more than one ink type. A dual-ink card shows two ink symbols and **counts as each ink type**.
- 1.10.1.3: some card abilities change deck construction, but only if the card could be in the deck in the first place. The rules' example is Christopher Robin – Hunny Sage, an Amethyst/Sapphire card. It can only go in an Amethyst/Sapphire deck, and its ability then lets other Hunny characters in regardless of ink.

### (c) At most 4 copies with the same full name: 1.10.1.1 and glossary

- 1.10.1.1: a deck can't contain more than 4 cards with the same full name.
- Glossary entry for "deck": a full name is the card's **name and version together**. So two cards that share a name but have different versions count as different cards. The glossary entries for character, item and location each repeat the 4-copy limit.

### (d) Mulligan ("altering" the hand): 2.2.1.4 to 2.2.3

- 2.2.1.4: each player draws 7 cards as their opening hand.
- 2.2.2: starting with the starting player and going in turn order, each player may alter their hand, **only once per game**.
- 2.2.2.1: the player puts any number of cards from their hand on the bottom of their deck without revealing them, then draws back up to 7.
- 2.2.2.2: each other player, in turn order, decides whether to alter and does the same.
- 2.2.2.3: anyone who altered 1 or more cards shuffles their deck and must let an opponent cut and/or shuffle it.
- 2.2.3: once everyone is done, the game starts with the starting player's turn.

Note: one fan page (a Happindie starter-deck guide, found in search results only and not cited) claimed you can only alter after game one. **The official rules don't say that.** 2.2.2 allows altering once in every game.

## 2. Community sources

Topic key: **INK** inkable ratio, **CURVE** cost curve, **ARCH** archetypes, **ROLES** card roles, **COPIES** copy counts, **MULL** mulligan.

### S1. Building a Winning Disney Lorcana Deck (official site, community-champion author)
- Author/site: Frank Karsten (Disney Lorcana Challenge champion), disneylorcana.com news
- Date: September 18, 2025
- URL: https://www.disneylorcana.com/en-US/news/2025/09/2025-9-10_deck_building_tips
- Topics: INK, CURVE, ARCH (aggro, control, midrange, songs theme, Sapphire ramp), ROLES, COPIES. Mulligan odds only.
- Claims:
  - Run **11–13 uninkables (47–49 inkables)**, and only use uninkable slots on cards that are good in every matchup.
  - Sample midrange curve: **8 one-drops, 8 two-drops, 12 three-drops, 8 four-drops, 8 five-drops, 4 six-drops, plus 12 actions/songs**. Aggro goes lower, control goes up to 8-ink plays, and Sapphire ramp shifts the curve.
  - Copies: **4** for essential cards, **3** for strong cards with diminishing returns, **2** for curve slots or 2–2 splits, and **1** for cards you want available but don't want to draw naturally. "Rule of Eight": 4 copies give about **40%** odds of seeing one in your opening 7 (**67%** with a full alter). Eight similar cards give **65%** (**90%** with a full alter). Run at least **4** card-advantage cards and at least **4** removal cards.

### S2. Lorcana Beginner's Guide: 5 Tips to Become a Better Player
- Author/site: Frank Karsten, Ultimate Guard blog
- Date: August 28, 2023
- URL: https://ultimateguard.com/en/blog/lorcana-beginners-guide-tips-frank-karsten
- Topics: MULL, INK, ROLES (removal, Evasive, Bodyguard), CURVE
- Claims:
  - Mulligan toward an ink curve: keep the best card at each cost, bottom duplicates, and bottom some uninkables if you hold **more than two**.
  - Don't alter aggressively if the deck is more than about **25% uninkable**.
  - In the starter decks, removal, ramp, Evasive characters and Bodyguards performed best. Protect your Evasive and Bodyguard characters because they guard your win conditions.

### S3. Lorcana Mulligan Guide: What to Keep & Bottom
- Author/site: Geeky Domain (no byline)
- Date: May 22, 2026
- URL: https://geekydomain.com/blogs/guides/lorcana-mulligan-guide
- Topics: MULL, ARCH (aggro, control/midrange, shift/combo, songs)
- Claims:
  - Inkables in the opening hand: **2 or fewer is a danger zone, 3–4 is healthy, 5 or more is ink-rich**. Look for one or two plays that cost **1–3**.
  - Two-question test: can you ink every turn, and do you have a meaningful play by turn 3? If not, alter.
  - On the play, favor proactive hands. On the draw, you can keep slightly greedier hands. Aggro should alter hard for a low curve, and song decks should balance singers against songs.

### S4. How to Build a Lorcana Deck: Beginner Deckbuilding Guide
- Author/site: Geeky Domain (no byline)
- Date: May 28, 2026
- URL: https://geekydomain.com/blogs/guides/building-your-first-60-card-lorcana-deck
- Topics: INK, CURVE, ARCH (aggro, midrange, control), ROLES, COPIES, MULL (briefly)
- Claims:
  - **10–13 uninkables**, with **10** suggested for new builders.
  - Curve: **1-cost 6–10, 2-cost 10–14, 3-cost 10–14, 4–5-cost 10–14, 6+ 4–8**. Card mix: about **22 characters, 12 actions/songs, 4 items/locations**. Aggro runs **24–26** characters, control **18–20** characters and **14–16** actions/songs.
  - Roles: Quester, Contester (for example Bodyguard), Interaction (removal), Engine (draw/ramp) and Finisher. Expensive finishers usually need only **2–3** copies.

### S5. Inkable vs Uninkable: Building Your Inkwell (Lorcana)

## 3. Consensus and disagreements

**Consensus**
- Run exactly 60 cards (S1, S4, S7), even though the rules set only a minimum.
- Most sources put uninkables somewhere between **10 and 15** for typical decks and say aggro can go higher. That puts inkables at about **45–50**.
- The curve peaks at 2–3 cost. Aggro goes lower, and control adds 7+ "bombs" plus more actions and songs.
- Use 4-ofs for core cards and fewer copies for expensive finishers or situational cards (S1, S4, S7).
- Altering costs no cards, so alter toward early plays and enough ink (S2, S3).

**Disagreements**
- **Uninkable count:**
  - S1 and S5 say 11–13 (47–49 inkables).
  - S4 says 10–13, with 10 for beginners.
  - S6 and S7 say 13–15 (45–47 inkables), and up to 18–20 for aggro.
  - S10 says at most 15–19.
  - S9's 40–50% inkable figure is an outlier.
- **Curve shape:** S1 peaks at 12 three-drops with only 4 six-drops. S7's sample peaks at 14 three-drops with 6 at 6+. S4 allows 6–10 one-drops, while S6 and S9 give 8–10 for costs 1 and 2 combined.
- **How many archetypes:** S1 names aggro, control, midrange and themes (Songs, Princesses, Evasives). S8 adds typal decks and treats ramp as part of control. Nobody agrees on a fixed list.
- **Uninkable threshold for mulligans:** S2 warns against aggressive alters above about 25% uninkable, while S10 accepts up to 15–19 uninkables (25–32%).
