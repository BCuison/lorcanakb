Chapter I

Rarities

Every card carries a rarity symbol at the bottom center, below the text box.[8] From Common up to Enchanted, each step adds a side to the shape.

RaritySymbolPull rateDescriptionValue (USD)
CommonGray circle6 per packMost of every set: the everyday cards that fill out a deck. Slots 1–6.$0.05–0.50
UncommonWhite open book3 per packSlightly scarcer staples. Slots 7–9.$0.10–1.50
RareBronze triangle≈ 1–2 per packFills most of the two Rare-or-better slots (10–11).$0.50–5
Super RareSilver diamond≈ 1:2–3Shares slots 10–11 with Rares and Legendaries.$1–20
LegendaryGold pentagon≈ 1:5–6The top regular rarity, often a set’s strongest or most famous characters.$2–60
EpicNot confirmed≈ 1:16New in Fabled. Frameless art, rainbow foil, holofoil icons and tinted text. Rarer than Legendary, more common than Enchanted. 18 per set.[1]$5–40
EnchantedRainbow hexagon≈ 1:96Full alternate art in foil, a chase version of a card from the set. Since Fabled the symbol is holofoil stamped.[1] Estimates run from 1:48 to 1:144.$40–800
IconicNot confirmed≈ 1:1,500New in Fabled and the rarest of all. A lore-star foil pattern with 3D hot stamping. 2 per set.[1][2]$700–4,000+

About these numbersRavensburger doesn’t publish pull rates.[3] The rates here are community estimates from opened boxes and cases,[4] written as cards to packs: 1:96 means about one card in every 96 packs. Values are typical prices for single, ungraded, near-mint cards in late 2026.[5][6][7] Popular cards sell well above their range, and foil versions of lower rarities usually cost a little more.

Chapter II

What’s in a pack

A booster pack holds twelve cards, and one of them is always foil.[3][4]

Slots 1–6
Commons.
Slots 7–9
Uncommons.
Slots 10–11
Rare or better: usually Rares, sometimes a Super Rare, Legendary or Epic.
Slot 12
The foil. Any rarity can turn up here, and it’s where Enchanted and Iconic cards appear.

A booster box holds 24 packs.
