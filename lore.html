Chapter I

The realm

Lorcana is a magical realm built around the Great Illuminary. You play an Illumineer, who uses magical ink to call up glimmers: ink-made versions of Disney characters.[1]

Great Illuminary
The heart of the realm, where glimmers are created.[1] Its Hall of Lorcana is where gathered lore belongs.[2]
Illumineers
Ink wielders, the players.[1] The official story names three it follows: Shanzay, Martin and Venturo.[3]
Glimmers
Characters made of ink.[1] Glimmers can be transformed into surprising new versions of themselves.[4]
Lore
What Illumineers race each other to gather.[1] Much of the story turns on lore being scattered or seized.[2]
Inklands
The unknown reaches of the realm below the Illuminary, where lost lore washes out.[4][2]
Floodborn
Glimmers changed by mixed flood ink. In the game, Floodborn are the characters with Shift.[3]
Chapter II

The Flood

Sets 1 to 3 · 2023 to 2024

The First Chapter

Set 1 · Sep 2023[5]

The realm opens its doors. Illumineers arrive at the Great Illuminary, gather a team of glimmers and compete with one another for lore, while Lorcana’s secrets wait to be uncovered.[1] The set is an introduction more than a plot.

Rise of the Floodborn

Set 2 · Dec 2023[5]

An explosion shakes the Great Illuminary. Magical ink mixes and floods its halls, and glimmers caught in it come out transformed, the first Floodborn. Precious lore is swept down into the Inklands.[4] The official story later calls the flood a mistake: mixed ink that was never meant to run loose.[3]

Into the Inklands

Set 3 · Mar 2024[5]

With lore scattered across the realm, the Illumineers head into the Inklands to bring it back to the Hall of Lorcana before someone else claims it.[2]

Chapter III

The Sea Witch

Sets 4 and 5 · 2024

Ursula’s Return

Set 4 · May 2024[5]

A storm has been gathering over Lorcana. A powerful Ursula glimmer, released from the Great Illuminary, finally sets her plan in motion: power over the whole realm.[6]

Illumineer’s Quest: Deep Trouble (May 2024) plays this fight out as a co-op scenario against an Ursula who grows stronger as the game goes on.[6]

Shimmering Skies

Set 5 · Aug 2024[5]

The realm throws a celebration. Illumineers and glimmers gather near the Great Illuminary for a festival, and Anna, Elsa and Olaf arrive back from an adventure in the Enchanted Forest. The Wreck-It Ralph racers join the fun.[7]

Chapter IV

The Hexwell Crown

Sets 6 to 8 · 2024 to 2025

Azurite Sea

Set 6 · Nov 2024[5]

Following an ancient map, the Illumineers sail out across the Azurite Sea. They face kakamora pirates and a whirlpool that opens onto a new mystery. Jim Hawkins, a pirate Stitch and Yzma are among the glimmers on board, and Big Hero 6 joins the game.[8]

Archazia’s Island

Set 7 · Mar 2025[5]

The voyage reaches Archazia’s Island, a haven full of friendly pets: the Aristocats, Bolt, Lady and the Tramp. Somewhere beyond them lies an ancient secret.[9]

Reign of Jafar

Set 8 · Jun 2025[5]

A Jafar glimmer takes over Archazia’s Island.[10] He seizes one half of the broken Hexwell Crown and hypnotizes a Basil glimmer into finding the other. He reforges the Crown in the island’s palace, gains great power and turns the island into a desert fortress, its throne room guarded by traps, illusions, statues and sandstorms. The Illumineers must break in and bring him down. Each booster carried a lore card, and the full set of 20 told the story of a rescue.[11]

Illumineer’s Quest: Palace Heist (Jun 2025) is the finale: steal the Crown and overthrow Jafar.[11] See our Palace Heist rules.

Chapter V

Whispers & Winter

Sets 9 to 11 · 2025 to 2026

Fabled

Set 9 · Sep 2025[12]

Fabled sits between story arcs. It brings back favorite cards from earlier sets alongside new ones, such as Goofy and Max from A Goofy Movie, and its only story text celebrates the power of storytelling.[13]

Whispers in the Well

Set 10 · Nov 2025[12]

A detective story. Strange, half-formed specters called Whispers start appearing, and Judy Hopps and Nick Wilde take the case. The Horned King looms as the villain, and Gargoyles, The Black Cauldron and DuckTales join the realm. Where the Whispers come from is left as a mystery, and Ravensburger said the set sets up the next arc.[12]

Winterspell

Set 11 · Feb 2026[14]

A mysterious vine starts growing in the Inklands. An Elsa glimmer tries to stop it by freezing it, but her magic sets off a winter storm across the realm.[15] Pocahontas, the Fox and the Hound cast, Stitch’s fellow experiments and Darkwing Duck weather it out.[15] The vine is the seed of the next crisis.[14]

Chapter VI

The Vine

Sets 12 and 13 · 2026

Wilds Unknown

Set 12 · May 2026[14]

Pixar glimmers arrive for the first time. Woody, Buzz and the Toy Story toys, the Incredibles and Merida’s clan from Brave are far from home and must cross dangerous country to get back.[16] While they trek back to the Great Illuminary, the vine soaks up all of the realm’s mixed flood ink for itself.[3]

Attack of the Vine

Set 13 · Jul 2026[17]

Full of flood ink, the vine grows out of control.[3] It reaches into the sky, traps glimmers and copies them as leafy Vinelings, such as a vine-made Ursula, Gaston and Pete, which live only to defend it. A garden party of glimmers heads deep into the roots, and the Monsters, Inc. crew fights to keep the vine off the Scare Floor; the casts of Up and Turning Red join in.[17] Vinelings are Floodborn from the start. With the flood ink gone, glimmers learn to team up across two inks to change form.[3]

Illumineer’s Quest: The Great Hunny Rescue is the co-op finale: Pooh and friends try to stop the Vine for good.[17]

Chapter VII

Hyperia City

Set 14 · 2026

Hyperia City

Set 14 · Oct 2026[18]

The story moves to a city where modern streets meet the fantastical, alive with music and art.[19] Its neighborhoods include City Center, the Wandering Straits, Old Towne Hyperia and the Marigold District, with the M.A.G.I.C. Academy at its edge. Coco joins the realm with Miguel and Héctor, and a new energy source, ink drops, powers the city. Judy Hopps, Nick Wilde and Honey Lemon start looking into the ink drops.[18]

Something sinister is stirring in the city’s alleys and waterways,[19] and someone wants the ink drops.[20] The official story hasn’t named who, or said whether this links back to the Vine.

Chapter VIII

What’s next

Announced by Ravensburger, Sep 2026[18]

Into the Inkdark
First set of 2027. A Hades glimmer is plotting something in the depths beneath the realm, and his fiery ambitions may raise a new threat.[18]
Cosmic Quest
Spring 2027. Ariel and Flounder, Stitch and his experiments, and WALL-E and EVE for the first time.[18]
Late 2027
A third set, not yet named, teased only as magical mayhem.[18]
