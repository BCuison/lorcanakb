Chapter I

Overview

A Jafar glimmer has reforged the legendary Hexwell Crown and made himself Supreme Sorcerer. The Illumineers race him for lore, but lore alone isn’t enough: you must also escape with the Reforged Crown before he steals it back.

Players
1 to 4 Illumineers on one team. The box has two prebuilt decks; a third or fourth player brings their own.
Your decks
Any deck that follows normal deckbuilding rules.
Jafar
Runs himself from a 50-card scenario deck. His play decisions are built into the rules.
Goal
Each Illumineer has 20 or more lore while one of you has the Reforged Crown. If Jafar reaches 40 lore, he takes the Crown and wins.

In the boxJafar card, 50-card scenario deck, two 60-card player decks, the Reforged Crown, two double-sided Battleground cards (four difficulties), playmat, Jafar draw token, 5 lore trackers, 29 damage counters and a sealed victory pack. Jafar’s cards have a unique back and aren’t tournament legal.

Chapter II

Setup

  1. Set Jafar’s draw token for the number of Illumineers (table below).
  2. Choose a Battleground and place it where everyone can see it. Start on Easy or Normal for your first few games.
  3. Place the Reforged Crown faceup in the Crown spot on Jafar’s playmat.
  4. Ink Moat: on Normal, Hard or Extreme, take Ink Moat out of Jafar’s deck and put it faceup in his Locations spot. On Easy, skip this; it stays in his deck.
  5. Shuffle Jafar’s scenario deck and place it in the Scenario Deck spot.
  6. Lore trackers to 0, one for Jafar and one per Illumineer. Each Illumineer shuffles and draws as normal; altering hands is allowed. Jafar doesn’t alter.
  7. Jafar goes first, and unlike a normal game, he draws on his first turn.

Chapter III

Jafar’s turn

  1. Ready all of Jafar’s cards.
  2. Set. Start-of-turn effects happen. If he has the Crown, he gains 2 lore. He gains lore equal to the total lore value of his locations.
  3. Resolve the row of his characters and items, from the Illumineers’ left to right. Items with an exert ability use it. Characters that can quest, quest. A character that can’t quest but has Reckless challenges; the Illumineers choose which of their characters defends. Otherwise the character does nothing.
  4. Draw one at a time, as many cards as his draw token shows. Keep them facedown in a pile next to his deck: this is his hand.
  5. Reveal his hand one card at a time and act on each before the next.

How Jafar plays a card

Ink check
He never pays ink; he only needs enough. If his inkwell holds at least the card’s cost, he plays it for free. If not, it goes into his inkwell facedown.
Characters, items
Go to the end of his row.
Locations
Go to the Locations spot on his playmat.
Actions
Resolve, then go to his discard.
Returned cards
A card returned to his hand joins the facedown pile and is revealed and played before anything he draws next turn.
Empty deck
Next time he has to draw, shuffle his discard into a new deck and keep drawing.
His choices
If an effect makes Jafar choose, the Illumineers decide together on his behalf. His cards say “Jafar” and “he” instead of “you.”
Chapter IV

Your turn

All Illumineers take their turns at the same time, using the normal turn. Coordinate: Support and “until end of turn” effects can go on each other’s characters.

“You”
On an Illumineer’s card, “you” and “your” mean the Illumineer who owns it. Jafar is everyone’s only opponent.
“Player”
An effect that targets a “player” can be used on Jafar.
Battleground
Pay the lore cost from your own total to use an ability. Each ability can be used only once per turn.
His locations
Obstacles block the Crown. Banish them by challenging, with Battleground damage, or with actions. Jafar can’t move characters to them.
Chapter V

The Crown

The Reforged Crown is an item that starts in Jafar’s play area and changes hands all game. Whoever has it in their play area at the start of their turn gains 2 lore. On your side, only the Illumineer holding it gains that lore.

Stealing it

  1. Jafar has no locations in play. Any location stops a steal.
  2. Every Illumineer exerts one ready character during their turn. Each must have been played on a previous turn.
  3. Choose one Illumineer together; they move the Crown to their play area.

The same steps move it from one Illumineer to another.

Jafar takes it back when

  • He plays Mechanical Snake or Palace Guard - Crown Hunter.
  • Iago - Crown Seeker quests during his turn.
  • The Crown would leave play for any reason, such as being banished.
  • He reaches 40 lore. He takes it immediately, then wins.

RememberWhile Jafar has the Crown, he can’t lose the game. If an effect would make him steal it while he already has it, it simply stays.

Chapter VIII

Tips

  1. Start on Easy or Normal. The booklet recommends it while you learn Jafar’s tricks.
  2. Break locations first. No steal is possible while any stand.
  3. Keep dry characters ready. Stealing needs one ready, non-new character from every Illumineer in the same turn.
  4. Hold the Crown on the right player. Only its holder gets the 2 lore, so pass it to whoever is furthest from 20.
  5. Mind his thresholds. His draw count goes up at 10, 20 or 30 lore depending on team size, and never comes back down.
  6. Build for Extreme. The booklet advises decks built for this quest.
