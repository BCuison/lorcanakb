Chapter I

Setup

Each player brings a deck of at least sixty cards in one or two inks. The first to gather twenty lore wins. End your turn with an empty deck and you lose, though drawing your last card is not fatal on its own.

Deck
60 cards or more. Up to four copies of any card with the same full name (name and version). One or two ink colors.
Opening
Shuffle, choose a first player, and draw seven. Once, you may put any number of cards on the bottom of your deck, draw that many, then shuffle.
Zones
Deck, hand, play, inkwell and discard. The inkwell is a face-down pile that pays for everything.
Chapter II

The turn

Beginning

  1. Ready your exerted cards.
  2. Set: start-of-turn effects happen, and each of your locations gives you its lore.
  3. Draw a card. The first player skips this on turn one.

Main

In any order, as often as you can pay:

  • Ink one card (once per turn)
  • Play characters, actions, items, locations
  • Quest
  • Challenge
  • Move a character to a location
  • Use abilities

End

  • End-of-turn effects happen.
  • An empty deck now means you lose.
  • Play passes to your opponent.
Chapter III

Ink

Once a turn, reveal a card that bears the inkwell symbol and lay it face down in your inkwell. Each card there is one ink. To play a card, exert ink equal to its cost; it all readies again next turn.

New in Hyperia CitySome cards create ink drops: counters in your inkwell. Remove one to pay 1 ink toward any cost. You can save them for later turns. More on ink drops.

Chapter V

Questing

Exert a ready, dry character and gain lore equal to its lore value. It stays exerted until your next turn, which leaves it open to challenge.

Locations add their lore at the start of your turn. Pay a location’s move cost to send a character inside.

Chapter VI

Challenges

  1. Choose your challenger. Exert one of your ready, dry characters.
  2. Choose a target. An opposing character that is exerted, or an opposing location.
  3. Trade blows. Each deals damage equal to its strength to the other, at the same time.
  4. Banish the fallen. Damage equal to or above willpower sends a character to the discard.

RememberDamage stays until healed or banished. Locations never deal damage back.

Chapter VII

Songs & Shift

Singing
Exert a ready, dry character whose cost is at least the song’s cost to play it for free. Or pay the ink as usual.
Sing Together
Exert any number of your ready characters whose costs add up to N or more.
Shift
Pay the Shift cost to play a character on top of one of yours with the same name. It keeps the damage and the ready or exerted state, and can act right away if the one beneath was dry. Newer variants, such as Duo Shift, are in Newer mechanics.

Chapter IX

Newer mechanics

How the mechanics from the latest sets work, newest last. Card text wins if it says otherwise.

Boost

Whispers in the Well · Nov 2025

Use it
During your turn, pay the Boost cost in ink to put the top card of your deck face down under that character. Each Boost ability works once per turn.
Cards under
Face-down cards under a character can’t be looked at by anyone, like your inkwell. They aren’t in play, and putting a card under something isn’t playing it.
Why bother
Cards reward it: some trigger whenever you put a card under them, some check whether there’s a card under them, and many from Winterspell on get stronger for each card underneath.
Leaving play
The whole stack moves together. If the character is banished, every card under it goes to the discard face up.

Watch your deckEvery Boost takes a card off the top of your deck. Since Comprehensive Rules 2.0 you lose if you end your turn with no cards in your deck.

Teams and new Shift

Attack of the Vine · Jul 2026

Team
A classification on dual-named characters such as Sulley & Boo. It counts as three names (Sulley, Boo, and Sulley & Boo) but is still one character.
Duo Shift
Play it on top of two of your characters at once, one with each name. For Mickey & Minnie, that’s a Mickey Mouse and a Minnie Mouse.
Combo Shift
Either kind: on one character with either name, or on two, one for each name.
Mixed states
Shifting onto a dry and a drying character makes the new one drying. Onto a ready and an exerted one, it’s exerted.
Not allowed
You can’t Duo Shift onto a single dual-named character. It’s one character, and Duo Shift needs two.
Temporary Shift
At the end of your turn, remove all damage from it and return only that card to your hand. The cards under it stay in play.
Classification Shift
Some Shifts name a classification or item instead of a character name. Temporary Red Panda Shift goes on any Red Panda; Potato Shift goes on an item named Potato.
Vinelings
Leafy copies made by the Vine. They’re printed Floodborn from the start, so they count as Floodborn without shifting.

Ink drops

Hyperia City · Oct 2026

What they are
Counters, not cards. When a card says you get an ink drop, put one in your inkwell. Each is worth 1 ink.
Spend them
Whenever you pay an ink cost, remove any number of drops to pay 1 ink each. Mix them with exerted ink cards, or skip them. A removed drop is gone.
Timing
Use them the turn you get them, or save them for a big turn later. There’s no limit on how many you hold.
Not paying ink
A cost that says “remove 2 ink drops”, like Baymax’s Shift, isn’t an ink payment. It doesn’t count for effects that care about paying ink.
Watch for
Some cards check whether you removed a drop to play them, such as Madam Mim and Intense Research. Every ink can make drops.

No tokens?Printed ink drop counters come in boosters. A die or coin works just as well.

Adventurous

Hyperia City · Oct 2026

A new keyword, the opposite of Reckless: the character can’t challenge and must quest each turn if able. Five older cards were reworded to use it: Cobra Bubbles - Dedicated Official, Ariel - Curious Traveler, Woody - Town Sheriff, Gaston - Frightful Bully and the action This Growing Pressure.

Still unclearThe official pages don’t yet say whether ink drops count as cards in your inkwell for effects that count them, or whether a shifted Team character counts as Floodborn. Check the Comprehensive Rules or a judge.

Chapter X

Glossary

Ready
Upright and usable.

Exert
Turn it sideways to use it. It stays that way until your next Ready step.

Drying
A character on the turn it’s played. Dry characters can quest, challenge and sing.

Banish
Remove from play to its owner’s discard.

Full name
Name plus version, such as “Mickey Mouse - Brave Little Tailor.” Deck limits use it; Shift uses the base name.

The bag
Where triggered abilities wait. The active player resolves theirs first.

Game state check
After every step and effect: has anyone reached 20 lore, and does any character have lethal damage?
