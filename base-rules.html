Chapter I

Setup

Each player brings a deck of at least sixty cards in one or two inks. The first to gather twenty lore wins. End your turn with an empty deck and you lose, though drawing your last card is not fatal on its own.

Deck
60 cards or more. Up to four copies of any card with the same full name (name and version). One or two ink colors.
Opening
Shuffle, choose a first player, and draw seven. Once, you may put any number of cards on the bottom of your deck, draw that many, then shuffle.
Zones
Deck, hand, play, inkwell and discard. The inkwell is a face-down pile that pays for everything.
Chapter II

The turn

Beginning

  1. Ready your exerted cards.
  2. Set: start-of-turn effects happen, and each of your locations gives you its lore.
  3. Draw a card. The first player skips this on turn one.

Main

In any order, as often as you can pay:

  • Ink one card (once per turn)
  • Play characters, actions, items, locations
  • Quest
  • Challenge
  • Move a character to a location
  • Use abilities

End

  • End-of-turn effects happen.
  • An empty deck now means you lose.
  • Play passes to your opponent.
Chapter III

Ink

Once a turn, reveal a card that bears the inkwell symbol and lay it face down in your inkwell. Each card there is one ink. To play a card, exert ink equal to its cost; it all readies again next turn.

New in Hyperia CitySome cards create ink drops: counters in your inkwell. Remove one to pay 1 ink toward any cost. You can save them for later turns.

Chapter IV

Cards

CharacterYesQuests for lore, challenges and defends. Stays until banished.
ActionNoOne effect, then to the discard. Songs are actions a character can sing for free.
ItemYesOngoing effects or abilities.
LocationYesLore each turn and bonuses for characters inside. Can be challenged.

Reading a character

Cost
Ink to play it.
Strength
Damage it deals in a challenge.
Willpower
Damage it can take before it’s banished.
Lore
Lore you gain when it quests.

DryingA character played this turn is still drying. It can’t quest, challenge, sing or use exert abilities until your next turn.

Chapter V

Questing

Exert a ready, dry character and gain lore equal to its lore value. It stays exerted until your next turn, which leaves it open to challenge.

Locations add their lore at the start of your turn. Pay a location’s move cost to send a character inside.

Chapter VI

Challenges

  1. Choose your challenger. Exert one of your ready, dry characters.
  2. Choose a target. An opposing character that is exerted, or an opposing location.
  3. Trade blows. Each deals damage equal to its strength to the other, at the same time.
  4. Banish the fallen. Damage equal to or above willpower sends a character to the discard.

RememberDamage stays until healed or banished. Locations never deal damage back.

Chapter VII

Songs & Shift

Singing
Exert a ready, dry character whose cost is at least the song’s cost to play it for free. Or pay the ink as usual.
Sing Together
Exert any number of your ready characters whose costs add up to N or more.
Shift
Pay the Shift cost to play a character on top of one of yours with the same name. It keeps the damage and the ready or exerted state, and can act right away if the one beneath was dry.
Chapter VIII

Keywords

All fourteen in the Comprehensive Rules.

Alertnew
Can challenge as if it had Evasive.

Bodyguard
May enter play exerted. Opponents must challenge it first if able.

Boost Nnew
Once during your turn, pay N to put your top card face down under it. Other abilities care about what’s underneath.

Challenger +N
+N strength while challenging.

Evasive
Only Evasive or Alert characters can challenge it.

Reckless
Can’t quest. Must challenge each turn if able.

Resist +N
Damage dealt to it is reduced by N.

Rush
Can challenge the turn it’s played.

Shift N
Pay N to play it on top of a same-named character.

Sing Together Nnew
Exert characters with total cost N or more to sing it.

Singer N
Counts as cost N for singing.

Support
When it quests, add its strength to another character this turn.

Vanish
Banished when an opponent’s action chooses it.

Ward
Opponents can’t choose it with effects. They can still challenge it.

Chapter IX

Glossary

Ready
Upright and usable.

Exert
Turn it sideways to use it. It stays that way until your next Ready step.

Drying
A character on the turn it’s played. Dry characters can quest, challenge and sing.

Banish
Remove from play to its owner’s discard.

Full name
Name plus version, such as “Mickey Mouse - Brave Little Tailor.” Deck limits use it; Shift uses the base name.

The bag
Where triggered abilities wait. The active player resolves theirs first.

Game state check
After every step and effect: has anyone reached 20 lore, and does any character have lethal damage?
