Chapter I

Identity

Strengths

  • High strength and willpower win challenges
  • Resist and Bodyguard keep the board alive
  • Direct damage removes threats

Weaknesses

  • Big bodies cost more, so starts can be slow
  • Evasive threats walk past ground blockers
  • Card draw usually comes from the partner ink
Chapter II

Signature mechanics

Resistrules →
Reduces damage taken, so characters survive repeated challenges.

Bodyguardrules →
Forces challenges onto a sturdy blocker.

Challengerrules →
Bonus strength while challenging.

Direct damage
Effects that deal damage outside challenges to finish off characters.

Shiftrules →
Omnidroid decks build up through bigger Shift forms.

Chapter III

Game plan

Turns 1 to 3

Hold the line

Play durable characters and Bodyguards. Don’t rush into bad trades.

Turns 4 to 6

Win the fights

Challenge with superior stats, use direct damage to finish characters and build board control.

Turns 7 and on

Grind it out

Quest with a board the opponent can’t profitably challenge.

InkingKeep your sturdy characters and removal; ink cards that only matter late.

Chapter VI

Facing Steel

  1. Go around, not through. Evasive characters quest past Steel’s blockers.
  2. Avoid fair fights. Steel usually wins straight challenges. Use effects instead.
  3. Pressure the slow start. Steel’s best bodies cost more. Get ahead on lore early.
  4. Track Resist. Resist reduces each instance of damage. Small hits may do nothing.
Chapter VII

Common mistakes

Playing too passively
Big blockers don’t win alone. Quest when it’s safe.

Ignoring Evasive threats
Plan answers for characters your board can’t challenge.

Leaving Bodyguard ready
Only exerted characters can be challenged, so a ready Bodyguard protects nothing. Play it exerted when you need cover now.

Running out of cards
Steel lacks draw. Don’t trade away your hand too fast.
