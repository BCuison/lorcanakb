Chapter I

Identity

Strengths

  • Rush and Challenger win challenges early
  • Strong removal through damage and banishing
  • Big, hard-hitting characters

Weaknesses

  • Reckless characters can’t quest
  • Works best with a partner ink that stabilizes
  • Runs out of steam if the opponent survives the early game
Chapter II

Signature mechanics

Rushrules →
Can challenge the turn it’s played, so it hits before the opponent can react.

Challengerrules →
Bonus strength while challenging. Wins trades it would otherwise lose.

Recklessrules →
Can’t quest and must challenge if able. Pure board pressure.

Removal
Damage and banish effects that clear blockers and threats.

Evasiverules →
Ruby pairs with Amethyst in Evasive builds that quest safely.

Chapter III

Game plan

Turns 1 to 3

Strike first

Play aggressive characters and start challenging early. Use Rush to punish anything they exert.

Turns 4 to 6

Clear the way

Remove blockers and engine pieces, then quest with what survives.

Turns 7 and on

Finish

Push the last lore before the opponent stabilizes. Don’t let a stalled board drag on.

InkingKeep Rush characters and removal; ink slow or expensive cards.

Chapter VI

Facing Ruby

  1. Don’t leave characters exposed. Exerted characters are Ruby’s favorite targets. Quest with what you can lose.
  2. Use Bodyguard and Resist. Sturdy blockers and damage reduction blunt early challenges.
  3. Survive to the late game. Ruby fades if it can’t close quickly. Trade efficiently and stabilize.
  4. Expect Rush. A freshly played Ruby character may challenge right away.
Chapter VII

Common mistakes

Taking bad trades
Challenging just because you can loses cards. Check strength and willpower first.

Forgetting to quest
Removal clears the way, but lore wins the game.

Reckless lock
Reckless characters must challenge. Plan for forced attacks.

Overcommitting
Keep threats in hand in case the board gets swept.
