Chapter I

Identity

Strengths

  • Draws extra cards and digs for answers
  • Bounce and recursion reset threats or reuse your best cards
  • Flexible across aggro, midrange and control

Weaknesses

  • Often relies on the partner ink for board presence
  • Card-draw engines take time to pay off
  • Bounce only buys time if the opponent can replay the card
Chapter II

Signature mechanics

Card draw
Amethyst’s core strength. More cards means more options and more ink.

Bounce
Return a character to its owner’s hand. Resets a big threat or removes a blocker for a turn.

Recursion
Cards that come back from the discard after they’re banished or used.

Evasiverules →
Several Amethyst archetypes win by questing with characters most opponents can’t challenge.

Shiftrules →
Omnidroid decks build up through bigger Shift forms.

Disruption
Effects that interfere with the opponent’s plans rather than fight head on.

Chapter III

Game plan

Turns 1 to 3

Set up

Play cheap characters and card draw. You’ll see more cards than most decks, so ink freely.

Turns 4 to 6

Take control

Bounce key blockers or threats, then use your card advantage to set up favorable challenges.

Turns 7 and on

Close it out

Replay your strongest threats and quest with Evasive characters the opponent can’t reach.

InkingAmethyst draws extra cards, so you can afford to ink more aggressively than most inks.

Chapter VI

Facing Amethyst

  1. Don’t overinvest in one threat. Bounce sends your big play back to hand. Spread value across the board.
  2. Pressure early. Card advantage snowballs. Make them spend cards on defense before the engine runs.
  3. Keep an answer for Evasive. Hold an Evasive or Alert character so their questers aren’t free lore.
  4. Watch the discard. Recursion means a banished card may not be gone for good.
Chapter VII

Common mistakes

Drawing instead of developing
Card draw is useless if you fall behind on board and lore.

Bouncing low-value targets
Save bounce for something expensive or for a lethal turn.

Shifting into removal
Check what the opponent can answer before you stack value on one character.

Forgetting the lore race
Amethyst can out-card the opponent and still lose to 20 lore.
