Amber

Songs, support and healing. Amber builds a board that gets stronger together and keeps it alive.

Chapter I

Identity

Strengths

  • Cheap singers play expensive songs early, for free
  • Abilities that trigger off your other characters reward going wide
  • Healing and protection keep key characters in play

Weaknesses

  • Not known for dealing damage; removal usually comes from songs or the partner ink
  • Wide boards are exposed to board sweepers
  • Singers exert to sing, which leaves them open to challenges
Chapter II

Signature mechanics

Singer and songsrules →
Singer N lets a cheap character sing a song costing up to N. Songs are Amber’s main source of removal and card draw.

Sing Togetherrules →
Big songs paid by exerting several characters at once. Fits a go-wide board.

Supportrules →
Adds a questing character’s strength to another character, setting up favorable challenges.

Healing
Removes damage, undoing your opponent’s chip damage and failed challenges.

Bodyguardrules →
Protects fragile engine characters by forcing challenges onto a sturdier body.

Board synergy
“Whenever you play a character” and similar triggers. More bodies, more value.

Chapter III

Game plan

Turns 1 to 3

Build the board

Play cheap characters and singers. Ink expensive or situational cards you can’t use soon. Keep at least one singer in hand.

Turns 4 to 6

Sing up the curve

Sing songs above your ink total to remove threats or draw cards. Shift into bigger versions. Quest with characters that won’t be punished.

Turns 7 and on

Close it out

Refill with draw songs, protect your lore engines and race to 20. Hold back if the opponent can sweep the board.

InkingKeep singers and your cheapest characters; ink duplicates of expensive cards and songs you can’t sing yet.

Pairings

Which second ink Amber goes with, and how often it shows up. The meta shifts with every set, so each side is dated.

Core Constructed

As of Oct 27, 2026 · incl. Set 14
  • Emerald: Royalty / AggroTop tier
    About a fifth of the field. Floods the board with cheap characters, ramps into Elinor early and overwhelms before the opponent stabilizes.
  • Sapphire: Detectives / PrincessesTop tier
    Rising with Hyperia City. Three Amber/Sapphire lists made the Top 8 of an Oct 24 event. Control leaning.
  • Amethyst: Ramp / MeridaTop tier
    Midrange. Ramps out strong characters early, then uses card draw to set up good challenges.
  • Steel: Lilo & Stitch / SongsPlayable
    Low share overall, but Lilo & Stitch took 2nd at an Oct 24 event. Cheap singers or Alien aggro.
  • Ruby: Boost controlNiche
    Builds a card-draw engine, then sweeps the board. Weak to fast starts.

Shares from inkdecks Core tier list (Oct 27). Archetype names from the official Sep writeup and Oct 24 results. Hyperia City (Set 14) is legal since its Oct 16 prerelease.

Chapter VI

Facing Amber

  1. Pressure the singers. A singer that exerts to sing becomes a challenge target. Make them pay for it.
  2. Finish what you damage. Healing resets chip damage, so commit to banishing a character rather than wounding it.
  3. Punish wide boards. Board sweepers and mass damage hit go-wide Amber hardest.
  4. Be fast against control builds. Boost and ramp decks need setup time. Early lore forces them to react.
Chapter VII

Common mistakes

Singing with a drying character
Singing is an exert action. A character played this turn can’t sing.

Overextending
Dumping your whole hand into a sweeper loses the game. Keep a reload in hand.

Inking your singers
Singers are your discount engine. Ink songs you can’t cast before you ink a singer.

Questing into open challengers
An exerted engine piece is a free target. Quest with what you can afford to lose.
