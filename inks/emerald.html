Chapter I

Identity

Strengths

  • Discard disrupts the opponent’s plans
  • Evasive and Ward characters are hard to challenge or target
  • Cheap characters make fast, wide starts

Weaknesses

  • Small characters lose fair challenges
  • Disruption alone doesn’t win; it needs a lore plan
  • Wide boards are exposed to sweepers
Chapter II

Signature mechanics

Discard
Makes the opponent discard cards, cutting off their best plays before they happen.

Evasiverules →
Only Evasive or Alert characters can challenge them, so they quest safely.

Wardrules →
Opponents can’t choose these characters with effects. They must challenge instead.

Card draw
Extra draws to fuel tricks and keep pressure on.

Go wide
Elinor - Renowned Diplomat rewards three or more exerted characters at the end of your turn.

Chapter III

Game plan

Turns 1 to 3

Swarm

Play several cheap characters. Early board presence powers Emerald’s payoffs.

Turns 4 to 6

Disrupt

Discard the opponent’s answers, quest with Evasive characters and avoid fair fights.

Turns 7 and on

Close it out

Keep the hand pressure on and race to 20 before they recover.

InkingKeep your cheap characters and disruption; ink expensive cards you won’t cast soon.

Chapter VI

Facing Emerald

  1. Keep an answer for Evasive. Hold an Evasive or Alert character, or their questers are free lore.
  2. Play around discard. Use cards rather than hoarding them. A full hand is a target.
  3. Punish wide boards. Sweepers and mass damage hit Emerald’s small characters hard.
  4. Challenge Ward characters. Ward stops effects, not challenges. Trade with them directly.
Chapter VII

Common mistakes

Discarding into an empty hand
Discard effects lose value when the opponent has few cards.

Taking fair fights
Small Emerald characters lose straight challenges. Quest instead.

Ignoring the lore race
Disruption buys time. Use it to get ahead on lore.

Overextending
Keep a reload in hand in case the board gets swept.
